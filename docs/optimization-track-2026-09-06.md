# Optimization track: what is next

**Audited 2026-09-06.** Six lanes swept the repo in parallel — previously-flagged open items, CI and guard
coverage, claim honesty, deployed-vs-git reality, the curriculum, and measured performance. Every candidate
was then handed to an independent agent instructed to refute it, and 38 survived.

**Nothing rated *high* after verification.** There is no fire. What follows is ranked by payoff per unit of
effort, and several verifiers *lowered* the severity their finder claimed — those corrections are kept in the
Verdict line rather than smoothed away.

## The recommendation

**Start with the two measured performance defects.** They are the only findings whose payoff is a number
rather than a judgement, both are bounded, and together they roughly halve the developer loop.

| # | Finding | Cost today | After the fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 | A backtracking regex in `web/__tests__/infra/credit-writers.test.ts:151` | **30,387 ms** — 65% of the web Jest suite's 45s wall clock | **4 ms**, the same 9 matches |
| 4 | `pytest` runs single-process and is 100% of CI's critical path | **655s** of a **12m22s** CI run; every other job is done at 2m19s | ~8x on 16 cores under `-n auto` |

Finding 18 is a one-line rewrite with an identical result set already proven by measurement. Finding 4 has one

**Then the two structural blind spots** — findings 2 and 14 — because both are cases where a guard exists and

### 2. A catastrophically-backtracking regex in one Jest suite costs 30s and is 65% of the web suite's wall clock

`medium` severity · `trivial` effort · Performance

**Evidence.** `web/__tests__/infra/credit-writers.test.ts:151` — `const CREDIT_WRITE = /ADD\s+credits\b|adds\.push\("credits |(?=[\s\S]*(?:`WALLET#\$\{|\bwalletKey\())[\s\S]*(?:update-item|UpdateItemCommand)|\bcreateWalletStore\b|\bapplyOnce(?:Retrying)?\b/;`. The third alternative's whole-file lookahead is retried at every start offset, so it is O(n²) per file. Measured directly against the suite's own scan (ROOTS = lambda, scripts, web/src, infra; 320 files, 2,183,314 bytes): current regex **30,387 ms**, 9 matches; the semantically identical split form `A.test(s) || (K.test(s) && U.test(s))` **4 ms**, the same 9 matches. Worst single file: `web/src/components/quantum/qpu-submit-panel.tsx` (67,498 bytes) at **4,611 ms**. Suite timings: `npx jest --silent` = 45.0s wall; the same run with `credit-writers` excluded = **15.8s**; the suite alone (5 assertions) = 31.4s. Its per-suite time in the full JSON report is 53.8s — the longest of 226 suites, 3.3× the next (`glossary.test.tsx`, 16.5s), against a median suite of 0.20s.

**Impact.** `npm test` takes 45s instead of ~17s locally, and the CI `Test` step 72s, because one suite's regex bounds the wall clock no matter how many workers Jest runs. The cost is quadratic in file size, so it grows faster than the codebase — and the same backtracking is a latent hang risk on a large generated file.

**Fix.** Replace the whole-file-lookahead alternative with two independent tests behind a helper: `const A = /ADD\s+credits\b|adds\.push\("credits |\bcreateWalletStore\b|\bapplyOnce(?:Retrying)?\b/, K = /`WALLET#\$\{|\bwalletKey\(/, U = /update-item|UpdateItemCommand/; const isCreditWriter = s => A.test(s) || (K.test(s) && U.test(s));` and call `isCreditWriter` at the three `CREDIT_WRITE.test(...)` sites. Verified equivalent: identical 9 matches over the scanned tree, and the suite's own four pinned samples at lines 189-200 still grade imports/rides/aliases true and reader false.

**Verdict.** Independently reproduced. (1) The regex is present and unfixed: web/__tests__/infra/credit-writers.test.ts:150-151 holds CREDIT_WRITE exactly as quoted, third alternative `(?=[\s\S]*(?:`WALLET#\$\{|\bwalletKey\())[\s\S]*(?:update-item|UpdateItemCommand)`. Last commit touching the file (12fa854) added the fourth alternative, not this one. (2) The cost reproduces on an exact replica of the suite's own scan (ROOTS line 79, SKIP_DIR/CODE lines 80-81, walk lines 83-103, the /\.test\.|__tests__|__fixtures__/ exclusion at line 159): 320 files / 2,189,734 bytes — matching the claimed 320 / 2,183,314 to char-vs-byte rounding — current regex 50,640 ms vs the split form 8.0 ms, with `identical matches: true` and the same 9 files (lambda/qpu/qpu-core.mjs, reconcile.mjs, lambda/stripe/{clawback,fulfillment,index,wallet-store}.mjs, lambda/tutor/index.mjs, scripts/founding-credit/issue.mjs, scripts/stripe/e2e-sandbox.mjs). Worst file web/src/components/quantum/qpu-submit-panel.tsx at 67,715 B / 4,400 ms, matching the claimed 67,498 B / 4,611 ms.
